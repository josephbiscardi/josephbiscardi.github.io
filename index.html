About

Hands-on systems and security professional focused on reliable, secure infrastructure.

I currently serve as IT Systems Specialist at Metallix Refining in Greenville, NC, where I support 130 users across a mixed device fleet. My day-to-day work covers Microsoft Entra ID and Intune, Mosyle MDM, Cisco Duo MFA, UniFi networking and Protect, SonicWall, SentinelOne, Datto backups, PRTG/Site24x7 monitoring, Windows Server, Linux, and Ignition SCADA for operational technology.

I enjoy building and refining systems—whether that’s automating provisioning, tightening identity and access, improving visibility, or keeping OT environments available and secure. Outside of work I run a homelab, build PCs, write scripts, and stay current with new technologies.

When I’m offline I spend time with friends and family, try new food, travel, and experience different cultures and cuisines (I also do occasional freelance travel agent work).
